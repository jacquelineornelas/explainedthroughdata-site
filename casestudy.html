The Process Improvement Project

For ninety days I documented real life without a pass or a fail attached to it. Every morning began with the same short set of measurements, and every evening closed with a written reflection. Three pillars run underneath the whole thing: Body, Career, and Relational and Spiritual. The documenting itself is not a fourth pillar. It is the discipline that makes the other three legible.

The frame is Lean Six Sigma, DMAIC, and statistical process control, applied to a person instead of a factory. That is not a metaphor. Every measurement carries an operational definition, every finding carries a special cause or common cause verdict, and every ruling is dated and defensible.

I registered eight predictions before I began, so that I could not move the goalposts once results started arriving. Four of them held and four of them did not. Both sets are published here, because a scorecard you cannot fail proves nothing.
